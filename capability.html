Company overview

Veteran-owned, family-owned excavation, concrete and site work contractor based in Franklin, Nebraska, established 2016. Insured for FEMA work, with completed FEMA storm recovery work and public project experience. Regularly works across south-central Nebraska and northern Kansas, and travels for large jobs.

Core competencies

  • Storm and FEMA recovery: debris cleanup, culverts, drainage, emergency site and road repair
  • Site preparation: excavation, trenching, grading, backfill
  • Water and utility lines: water lines, line repair, stock waterers
  • Poured concrete: pads, aprons, feedlot runways, retaining walls
  • Gravel and roads: hauling, spreading, regrading
  • Irrigation: lawn sprinkler installation and repair
