FAQ

Common questions.

How far do you travel?

We're based in Franklin, Nebraska, regularly work across south-central Nebraska and northern Kansas, and have the ability to travel for large jobs. Mileage is figured into the quote, so call Gina to see if your job is in range.

Do you install waterers?

Yes. We set the waterer, run the water line and pour the concrete around it.

Can you pour feedlot concrete?

Yes. Pads, aprons, alleys, runways and bunk concrete built for cattle traffic.

Do you take government jobs?

Yes. BG Welsh is insured for FEMA work and has done storm cleanup under a FEMA contract and site work helping build an FSA office. See the government and FEMA page.
